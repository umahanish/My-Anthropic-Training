# Effort Estimation — Campus Task & Study Group Manager

## Method
Story points (relative sizing) converted to hours using a rough guide for a
**learning developer** (slower than an experienced dev, since time includes
research/learning, not just typing code):

- 1 point ≈ 2 hrs
- 2 points ≈ 4 hrs
- 3 points ≈ 6-7 hrs
- 5 points ≈ 10-12 hrs
- 8 points ≈ 16-18 hrs (consider splitting into smaller stories)

| **Total** | **66** | **~130 hrs** | **~11-12 sprints (6 months)** |

## Assumptions & risks
- Assumes ~10-15 hrs/week of focused time; adjust sprint count if actual
  availability differs.
- First 2 sprints (auth, tooling) usually run slower than estimated for a
  first-time full-stack builder — budget slack, don't compress it.
- Mobile epic (E6) risk: environment setup (Expo, emulator/device pairing)
  is a common time sink not captured in "coding" hours — the sprint plan
  already gives it 2 full sprints for this reason.
- Re-estimate after Sprint 2: by then actual velocity (points/sprint) is
  known and the rest of the plan should be recalibrated against it rather
  than the original guess.
